Publications

  • 2026.12.15
    Self-Supervised Keyframe Discovery for Horizon-Invariant Behavior Cloning
    NeurIPS 2026 | Prabin Kumar Rath, Omkar Patil, Nakul Gopalan
    Self-supervised method that discovers information-critical observations (mnemonics) as keyframes, conditioning behavior cloning policies on them to retain context over long horizons, without annotations.
  • 2026.10.15
    StageCraft: Execution Aware Mitigation of Distractor and Obstruction Failures in VLA Models
    IROS 2026 | Kartikay Pangaonkar*, Prabin Rath*, Omkar Patil*, Nakul Gopalan
    Training-free VLM-based scene staging for VLAs (π0.5, SmolVLA); +40% absolute success on real robots.
  • 2026.05.15
    Factorizing Diffusion Policies for Observation Modality Prioritization
    ICRA 2026 | Omkar Patil, Prabin Rath, Kartikay Pangaonkar, Eric Rosen, Nakul Gopalan
    Method to prioritize observational modalities, such as vision over tactile, for learning diffusion policies.
  • 2026.03.15
    You've Got a Golden Ticket: Improving Generative Robot Policies With A Single Noise Vector
    arXiv | Omkar Patil, Ondrej Biza, Thomas Weng, Karl Schmeckpeper, Wil Thomason, Xiaohan Zhang, Kausik Sivakumar, Robin Walters, Nakul Gopalan, Sebastian Castro, Stephen Hart, Eric Rosen
    Search method to find a single constant noise vector (golden ticket) that improves pretrained diffusion/flow matching robot policies including VLAs (GR00T N1.5, π0, SmolVLA) without any retraining.
